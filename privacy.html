2.1 Contact Forms

When you submit an enquiry through our website, we may collect:

  • Name

  • Telephone number
  • Service requested
  • Property or project information you choose to provide
  • Message content
  • Other information you voluntarily submit

2.2 Chatbot

If you use our website chatbot or AI assistant, we may receive and process the messages and information you choose to provide.

This may include:

4. Lawful Bases for Processing

Depending on the circumstances, we may process personal data under one or more of the following lawful bases:

Contract

We may need to process your information to provide a service you have requested or to take steps at your request before entering into a contract.

For example, this may include preparing a quotation or arranging an appointment.

Legal Obligation

We may process information where necessary to comply with a legal or regulatory obligation, including accounting and tax requirements.

Legitimate Interests

We may process information where necessary for our legitimate business interests, provided those interests do not override your rights and interests.

This may include operating and securing our website, managing enquiries, maintaining business records and preventing fraud or misuse.

Consent

Where applicable, we may rely on your consent.

Where processing is based on consent, you may generally withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing that took place before consent was withdrawn.

10. How Long We Keep Personal Data

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including legal, accounting and business requirements.

Enquiries

We currently intend to retain relevant financial records for approximately 6 years, subject to applicable legal and accounting requirements.

Different information may be retained for different periods depending on its purpose.
